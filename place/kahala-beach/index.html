Kahala Beach

A quiet, uncrowded upscale beach stretching along Kahala Avenue, reached through public shoreline access paths between homes. Shallow and calm, good for wading.

Kahala Avenue (public access paths), Honolulu, HI 96816

Dog rules

Leashed dogs are allowed on this state-jurisdiction residential shoreline, which is not a City beach park and carries no sanctuary designation.

Hours

Open daily, dawn to dusk (public shoreline access)

Amenities

  • Street parking
  • Public beach access paths
  • Calm swimming

Dog rules on Oʻahu

Leashed dogs are allowed only at parks and beaches the City & County of Honolulu has specifically designated; there is no blanket permission.

Common questions

Are dogs allowed at Kahala Beach?

Yes. Kahala Beach is open to leashed dogs. Leashed dogs are allowed on this state-jurisdiction residential shoreline, which is not a City beach park and carries no sanctuary designation.

What are the hours at Kahala Beach?

Open daily, dawn to dusk (public shoreline access)

Where is Kahala Beach?

Kahala Avenue (public access paths), Honolulu, HI 96816, East Honolulu, Oʻahu. Coordinates 21.2683, -157.7802.
