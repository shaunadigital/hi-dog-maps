Polo Beach Park

A sandy beach at the south end of the Wailea Beach Path, with a small grassy picnic area beside the resort. Expect other beachgoers on the sand.

4100 Wailea Alanui Dr, Wailea, HI 96753

Dog rules

Maui County requires dogs be leashed (10 ft max leash) at county parks and beaches. This county beach park is maintained by the Wailea Community Association; the county leash rule still applies. Pick up after your dog and check posted signs.

Dog rules on Maui

The most permissive regime in the state: leashed dogs are welcome by default in Maui County parks and on county beaches, on a lead of 10 feet or less.

Common questions

Are dogs allowed at Polo Beach Park?

Yes. Polo Beach Park is open to leashed dogs. Maui County requires dogs be leashed (10 ft max leash) at county parks and beaches. This county beach park is maintained by the Wailea Community Association; the county leash rule still applies. Pick up after your dog and check posted signs.

Open daily (check posted hours)
