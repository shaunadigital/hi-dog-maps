Bill and Peggy Paty Kaiaka Bay Beach Park

A quiet 53-acre wooded beach park on Kaiaka Bay west of Haleʻiwa town, with campsites and shady ironwood trees.

66-449 Haleʻiwa Rd, Haleʻiwa, HI 96712
