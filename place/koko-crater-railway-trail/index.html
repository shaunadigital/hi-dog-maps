Koko Crater Railway Trail

A grueling 1.8-mile out-and-back straight up an old military tramway of over 1,000 railroad ties to the summit of Koko Crater, rewarding hikers with 360-degree views of Hawaiʻi Kai and Hanauma Bay.

Koko Head District Park, 7604 Koko Head Park Road, Honolulu, HI 96825

Dog rules

Sources conflict on whether dogs are permitted, and even where allowed the steep, exposed railroad ties and lack of shade or water make it hazardous for dogs; verify current policy before bringing a dog.

Hours

Sunrise to sunset (daily)

Amenities

  • Park parking
  • Restrooms at base park
  • No shade
  • No water on trail

Common questions

Are dogs allowed at Koko Crater Railway Trail?

Yes. Koko Crater Railway Trail is open to leashed dogs. Sources conflict on whether dogs are permitted, and even where allowed the steep, exposed railroad ties and lack of shade or water make it hazardous for dogs; verify current policy before bringing a dog.

What are the hours at Koko Crater Railway Trail?

Sunrise to sunset (daily)

Where is Koko Crater Railway Trail?

Koko Head District Park, 7604 Koko Head Park Road, Honolulu, HI 96825, East Honolulu, Oʻahu. Coordinates 21.2818, -157.6947.
