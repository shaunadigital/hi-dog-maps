Laupāhoehoe Civic Building

A county civic building in the town of Laupāhoehoe on the Hāmākua coast, near Laupāhoehoe Point Beach Park and the Laupāhoehoe Pool.

Laupāhoehoe, HI 96764

Dog rules

Leashed dogs are allowed. Hawaiʻi County Code § 4-4-29 permits dogs in County parks when kept on a leash no longer than six feet. Note that dogs are barred from County beach parks on this island entirely, even leashed; this is a regular county park, not a beach park.

Hours

Open daily, dawn to dusk (county park)

Amenities

None listed

Dog rules on Hawaiʻi Island

A hybrid, and the reason you can't generalise from another island: leashed dogs are fine in county parks on a 6-foot lead, but are banned outright from every county beach park, even leashed.
