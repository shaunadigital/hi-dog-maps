Common questions

Are dogs allowed at Kula Park?

Yes. Kula Park is open to leashed dogs. Maui County requires dogs be leashed (10 ft max leash) at all public parks and beaches by default; there's no per-park designation list like Oʻahu's, it's the county-wide baseline rule. Pick up after your dog and check posted signs.

What are the hours at Kula Park?

Open daily, dawn to dusk

Where is Kula Park?

Lower Kula Rd, Kula, HI 96790, Upcountry, Maui. Coordinates 20.75931, -156.33007.
