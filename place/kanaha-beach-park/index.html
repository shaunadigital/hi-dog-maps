Kanahā Beach Park

A long beach park beside Kahului Airport with shaded picnic areas and plenty of parking. It is Maui's main windsurfing and kitesurfing launch, so the sand gets busy with gear on windy afternoons.

📍 Amala Pl, Kahului, HI 96732

🐶 Dog rules

Maui County requires dogs be leashed (10 ft max leash) at county parks and beaches. Watch for kite lines and boards on the sand, and keep your dog clear of launch areas. Pick up after your dog and check posted signs.

🕐 Hours

Open daily, 7am to 8pm (check posted hours)

  • Parking

Dog rules on Maui

The most permissive regime in the state: leashed dogs are welcome by default in Maui County parks and on county beaches, on a lead of 10 feet or less.
