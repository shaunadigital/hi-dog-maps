Judd Trail (Judd Memorial Loop)

A shady 1.2-mile loop through Nuuanu Valley's bamboo and eucalyptus groves, passing the Jackass Ginger swimming hole. An easy, family-friendly forest walk close to town.

Judd Trailhead, 4022 Nuuanu Pali Drive, Honolulu, HI 96817

Dog rules

Dogs are allowed but must remain on a leash; the connecting Nuʻuanu Trail does NOT allow dogs, so stay on the Judd loop.

Hours

Sunrise to sunset (daily)

Amenities

  • Roadside parking
  • Stream
  • Shade
  • Swimming hole
  • No restrooms

Are dogs allowed at Judd Trail (Judd Memorial Loop)?

Yes. Judd Trail (Judd Memorial Loop) is open to leashed dogs. Dogs are allowed but must remain on a leash; the connecting Nuʻuanu Trail does NOT allow dogs, so stay on the Judd loop.
