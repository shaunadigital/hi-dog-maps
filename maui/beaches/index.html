The beach rule on Maui

The most permissive regime in the state: leashed dogs are welcome by default in Maui County parks and on county beaches, on a lead of 10 feet or less.

Common questions

Are dogs allowed on Maui beaches?

The most permissive regime in the state: leashed dogs are welcome by default in Maui County parks and on county beaches, on a lead of 10 feet or less.

Which Maui beaches allow dogs?

This map lists 25 on Maui, grouped by area: South Maui (13), West Maui (5), North Shore (4), Central Maui (3). Each has its own page with the leash rule, hours and directions.

Why are some Maui beaches marked "verify before visiting"?

3 of these allow dogs only on the wet sand below the high-tide line, or their rule couldn't be fully confirmed from an official list. Check posted signs when you arrive.

Can dogs go on Hawaiʻi state park beaches?

No. State-park beaches are closed to dogs on every island, leashed or not, service animals excepted.
