The beach rule on Oʻahu

Leashed dogs are allowed only at parks and beaches the City & County of Honolulu has specifically designated; there is no blanket permission.

Common questions

Are dogs allowed on Oʻahu beaches?

Leashed dogs are allowed only at parks and beaches the City & County of Honolulu has specifically designated; there is no blanket permission.

Which Oʻahu beaches allow dogs?

This map lists 57 on Oʻahu, grouped by area: Windward (17), North Shore (16), Leeward (15), East Honolulu (8), Honolulu (1). Each has its own page with the leash rule, hours and directions.

Why are some Oʻahu beaches marked "verify before visiting"?

46 of these allow dogs only on the wet sand below the high-tide line, or their rule couldn't be fully confirmed from an official list. Check posted signs when you arrive.

Can dogs go on Hawaiʻi state park beaches?

No. State-park beaches are closed to dogs on every island, leashed or not, service animals excepted.
