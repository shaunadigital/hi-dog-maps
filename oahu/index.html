The rule on Oʻahu

Leashed dogs are allowed only at parks and beaches the City & County of Honolulu has specifically designated; there is no blanket permission.

Honolulu runs a designation list rather than a default rule: a park or beach allows dogs only if it appears on the City & County's list, which expanded to 72 parks in February 2026. Anywhere not on it, dogs are prohibited even on a leash.

Several Oʻahu beaches allow dogs only below the high-tide line, makai of the debris line, not on the dry upland sand. Those are flagged on this site rather than presented as straightforwardly open.

Oʻahu also has the state's largest set of fenced off-leash dog parks, which are free and city-run.

Common questions

Are dogs allowed on Oʻahu beaches?

Leashed dogs are allowed only at parks and beaches the City & County of Honolulu has specifically designated; there is no blanket permission.

How many dog-friendly places are there on Oʻahu?

This map lists 152 on Oʻahu: 15 dog parks, 53 parks, 57 beaches, 9 trails, 18 restaurant patios.

Are Hawaiʻi state park beaches open to dogs?

No. State-park beaches are closed to dogs on every island, leashed or not, with service animals excepted. That applies regardless of what the county rule says.
